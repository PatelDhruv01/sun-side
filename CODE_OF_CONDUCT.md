# Code of conduct

Sun Side follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome people whatever their background or
experience, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks aren't acceptable anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, reviews or comments.

If something goes wrong, raise it privately with the maintainer,
[PatelDhruv01](https://github.com/PatelDhruv01). Reports are handled in confidence.
